Admin Product Guide

This guide explains how to manage products in the Rishi's Lily Farm admin panel.

Getting Started

Navigate to src/admin/products.html to access the product manager. You'll need internet access for Firebase to load.

Adding a Product

1
Click "Add Product" — the green button at the top-right of the products page.
2
Enter details — product name, category (Lily / Exotic / Fertilizer), price in TTD, description, and fertilizer info. A slug is auto-generated from the name.
3
Image URL — enter the path relative to the site root (e.g. img/purple-blue-day-bloomer.jpg).
4
Stock & Featured — toggle "In Stock" to control availability. Toggle "Featured" to show it on the homepage featured section.
5
Save — the product is stored in Firestore and instantly appears on the public site.

Editing / Deleting

Each product card has Edit and Delete buttons. Editing opens the same form pre-filled. Deleting shows a confirmation dialog.

Categories

  • Lily — water lilies, lotus, and aquatic plants
  • Exotic — tropical ornamentals like heliconia, amaryllis, champaca
  • Fertilizer — plant care products like fertilizing tablets ($20)

Fertilizer Info

The Fertilizer Information field on each product tells customers what fertilizer to use and how often. The Fertilizing Tablet product itself is a $20 item in the Fertilizer category that customers can buy separately.

Featured Products

Products marked as Featured appear in the "Featured Lilies" section on the homepage. Mark your best-selling or new arrivals as featured to promote them.

Need More Help?

Contact the developer or check the Firebase console for database management.
