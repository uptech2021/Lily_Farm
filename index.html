Our story

Grown in Trinidad,
shared with love.

Rishi's Lily Farm is a family-owned farm in Gasparillo specialising in rare lilies and exotic plants. What began in 2019 as a love for remarkable blooms has grown into a place where local gardeners can discover something truly special.

Each plant is nurtured in our tropical climate, selected by hand and prepared with care before it makes its way to your garden.

View all FAQs
What types of lilies do you offer?

We grow tropical water lilies, night and day bloomers, lotus varieties and a rotating collection of exotic plants.

What are your delivery charges?

Delivery is $60 in Central and South Trinidad and $85 in North Trinidad. Orders over $500 qualify for free delivery.

Are your plants naturally grown?

Yes. We focus on responsible care and healthy growing practices suited to Trinidad's tropical climate.

Can I place an order for pickup?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a convenient pickup time from our Gasparillo farm.
